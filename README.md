## 👋 About me

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science student at VIT-AP, specializing in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning. My work sits at the intersection of NLP research and practical AI engineering:

- **Research** — adaptive-depth transformers and temporal knowledge graphs for discovering emerging research gaps.
- **LLM systems** — RAG pipelines, multi-agent workflows with guardrails, and the Model Context Protocol.
- **Shipping things** — FastAPI services, vector databases, and MLOps practices that turn a notebook into something people can actually use.

I care about evidence over claims: experiments, ablation studies, and code you can inspect.

## 🚀 Featured work

| Project | What it does | Stack |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Temporal Knowledge Graph for Emerging Research Gaps](https://github.com/YasaswiAnjan/Temporal-graph) | A multi-stage pipeline that builds yearly knowledge graphs from scientific papers, extracts entities and relations, and ranks unlinked concept pairs as likely future research gaps. Covers 1,400 NLP and 840 COVID-19 papers, with a Streamlit dashboard for exploration. | SciBERT · NetworkX · FAISS · Node2Vec · SPECTER |
| [CRADT — Compute-Regularized Adaptive Depth Transformer](https://github.com/YasaswiAnjan/CRADT) | A transformer that chooses how many layers to use **per input**. Each layer has a sigmoid halting head, training uses a differentiable soft-depth penalty, and the model warm-starts new layers from 2 to 4 when learning stalls. Benchmarked on SST-2, IMDB and Yelp at 0.421 ms per sample. | PyTorch · Transformers · Hugging Face Datasets |
| **AI Hallucination Detection & Production Systems** *(in progress)* | Detecting LLM hallucinations with statistical inconsistency checks and transformer confidence scores, wrapped in a multi-agent RAG workflow with evaluation loops and safety guardrails, deployed as a full-stack API. | Python · Transformers · Vector DBs · FastAPI |

**Other things I've built:** [Enter-The-Matrix](https://github.com/YasaswiAnjan/Enter-The-Matrix) — a dependency-free HTML5 canvas digital-rain animation — and [plytek](https://github.com/YasaswiAnjan/plytek) — a static site for a plywood and timberwood brand.
